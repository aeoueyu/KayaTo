INTRODUCTION

        Oral health is a fundamental component of overall health and well-being, directly impacting
an individual’s quality of life [1]. As the healthcare industry modernizes, the adoption of specialized
health information technology has become essential for clinical efficiency and improved patient
care [2]. Dental Information Management Systems have emerged to help transition clinics from
traditional to streamlined digital environments [3]. These systems facilitate the secure management
of Electronic Medical Records (EMR), patient history, inventory, and scheduling, thereby reducing
administrative burdens and allowing dental practitioners to focus primarily on patient treatment [4].

        Despite growing technology and digitalization, many startup and expanding dental clinics
still rely on manual methods for their daily operations [2]. This often results in disorganized patient
records, scheduling conflicts, and inefficient clinic management [4]. Furthermore, patients
frequently experience anxiety or hesitation due to a lack of accessible, personalized dental health
information [5]. Integrating Artificial Intelligence (AI) for educational patient engagement and
recommended scheduling addresses this gap, providing patients with a clearer understanding of
their oral health and fostering higher trust and satisfaction [6].

        Addressing these operational and patient engagement challenges is a highly necessary and
impactful step for growing dental practices. Developing an integrated platform that automates clinic
workflows, such as centralizing electronic medical records (EMR), modernizing appointment
scheduling, and utilizing artificial intelligence for patient education and recommended scheduling,
can significantly resolve existing operational problems. Various studies provided evidence that the
implementation of specialized information systems across different medical and dental clinics has
greatly improved overall clinical efficiency [3], reduced administrative errors [4], and elevated the
quality of patient care [7]. Therefore, applying these technological innovations is important in
bridging the gap between traditional manual operations and modernized dental healthcare delivery.




1.1 PROJECT CONTEXT

        The United Nations’ Sustainable Development Goal 3 emphasized the importance of
ensuring healthy lives and promoting well-being for all at all ages [8]. A key factor in achieving this
goal is the accessibility, efficiency, and quality of healthcare delivery, including oral health. To fulfill
this objective globally, the healthcare industry is actively transitioning from traditional practices to
digital health information systems to improve patient outcomes and clinic operations [7]. However,
in developing countries like the Philippines, dental practice modernization remains in transition.
While top-tier hospitals have fully embraced digitalization, many local and expanding dental clinics
still encounter barriers to digital transformation, leading to operational inefficiencies that directly
affect patient care and clinic management [9]. Locally, expanding a dental clinic into a multi-branch
enterprise introduces significant operational challenges. When clinics rely heavily on manual
procedures and fragmented communication platforms, they face issues such as isolated data silos
and insecure transfer of sensitive medical records. Without unified digital infrastructure, both the
administrative efficiency of the clinic and the overall engagement and satisfaction of the patients are
compromised [10].

       Dentime Dental Clinic, established in 2024, is a rapidly growing dental practice located at
2/F JN Terraces Bldg., 881 J. P. Rizal Street, Brgy. Concepcion Uno, Marikina City, operating with a
floor area of 30 square meters. Dr. Michael Edwin Tamayo, DMD, owns the clinic. Currently, the clinic
has one in-house dentist, one secretary, and a clinic manager, and it exclusively offers dental
services. Serving about 6 to 10 patients daily through scheduled appointments and walk-ins, the
clinic has seen a steady patient flow. This inspired management to expand operations to
accommodate and treat more patients from other communities by constructing a second branch at
2/F Arquilos Commercial Bldg., Phase 1 Block 12 Lot 3 Eastwood Residences, San Isidro, Rodriguez,
Rizal. To support the expansion, management plans to hire additional dentists as needed, depending
on the initial patient volume at the new location. While there are no concrete plans for additional
branches yet, the management estimates that future clinics would also require around 30 to 50
square meters of space to support their operations effectively.

       Currently, Dentime Dental Clinic relies on manual, fragmented methods to manage daily
operations. The clinic accommodates walk-ins and schedules patient appointments primarily
through its Facebook Page and Messenger. The clinic then tracks these appointments using Google
Calendar and Excel spreadsheets. The clinic stores patient histories and dental records manually in
paper files and folders. Furthermore, the clinic does not formally record dental materials used during
treatments, relying only on visual checks.

       As the clinic serves more patients, its current appointment setup creates significant
challenges. While the clinic manages to avoid overlapping schedules, relying on different platforms
like Facebook Messenger for appointments and follow-up reminders, alongside Google Calendar
and Excel for tracking, requires significant manual effort from the limited staff. This fragmented
communication and tracking setup compromises optimal clinical efficiency.

       Furthermore, the ongoing construction of the second branch highlights a critical limitation in
records management. With dental records currently stored in physical folders, transferring patient
histories and treatment records between the main clinic and the new branch will have to be done
manually. Without a centralized digital database, retrieving and sharing these physical records
across locations will cause operational delays, risk data misplacement, and lead to fragmented
medical histories for patients visiting multiple branches.

       Another significant operational challenge is manually monitoring clinic inventory. Currently,
the clinic relies solely on visual checks, with no formal record to track the use of fast-moving and
perishable dental supplies such as cotton, anesthesia, brackets and wires for braces, needles,
syringes, gloves, and masks. Without system-assisted inventory tracking, staff struggle to accurately
monitor stock levels after treatments, increasing the risk of supply shortages during critical
procedures.

       To address these issues, the researchers propose developing NgitiFy, a centralized Dental
Information Management System. NgitiFy will replace the clinic’s fragmented scheduling process
with a dedicated Appointment and Schedule Management Module, allowing for seamless booking
and automated notifications. Furthermore, the system will introduce a centralized Electronic
Medical Record (EMR) module capable of secure, branch-level data isolation. This ensures that
patient files can be safely accessed and transferred between the first and second branches without
relying on insecure messaging platforms [3]. Additionally, the system includes a comprehensive
dashboard and report generator to track daily patient volume, providing management with essential
data to support and justify decisions about future branch expansion.

       Beyond administrative efficiency, NgitiFy provides Dentime Dental Clinic with a strong
competitive advantage through its AI-Driven Patient Engagement Module. To promote patient
awareness and proactive oral care, the system features AI Patient Engagement that provides
personalized dental health advice, such as dietary recommendations for teeth maintenance, as well
as recommended visit windows for follow-up schedules. Moreover, the AI-Assisted Radiograph
Review feature within the Patient EMR allows dentists to review radiograph quality, record clinical
findings and annotations, and prepare dentist-approved summaries for patients. Lastly, the AI
Patient Engagement Module supports continuous oral health management by providing access to
NgitiBot, Dental Health Education, Oral Health Management, and the Recommended Visit Window,
helping patients maintain awareness of their daily oral health. Ultimately, NgitiFy will empower
Dentime Dental Clinic to transition from manual, single-branch operations to a highly efficient,
multi-branch digital ecosystem, ensuring high-quality service delivery as they continue to expand.
1.2 PURPOSE AND DESCRIPTION

       The project, NgitiFy: A Dental Information Management System with AI-Driven Patient
Engagement for Dentime Dental Clinic, aims to develop a centralized digital platform that improves
patient record management, clinic operations, and patient engagement. This project addresses the
clinic’s current reliance on manual, non-integrated systems such as walk-in scheduling, Facebook
inquiries, and Google Calendar tracking. These methods often create inefficient workflows,
fragmented patient histories, and challenges in monitoring appointments and managing clinic
inventory. By implementing NgitiFy, the clinic can centralize patient data, automate administrative
tasks, and improve overall efficiency, especially as Dentime Dental Clinic expands to multiple
branches.

       NgitiFy introduces several relevant features, including an Electronic Medical Record,
Interactive Digital Odontogram, AI-Assisted Radiograph Review, and AI Patient Engagement Module.
The AI Patient Engagement Module consists of NgitiBot, Dental Health Education, Oral Health
Management, and the Recommended Visit Window. NgitiBot provides supported conversational
oral health guidance and personalized dietary recommendations. Dental Health Education provides
approved educational materials, while Oral Health Management allows patients to record and
monitor relevant oral health information. The Recommended Visit Window provides visit guidance
based on available patient records and oral health inputs. The system also includes appointment
scheduling and supply and stock monitoring to improve clinic operations.

       The system is comprehensively designed to improve service delivery and administrative
operations. Specifically, the development and implementation of NgitiFy will be highly beneficial to
the following:

       Dental Patients. It provides convenient appointment booking, access to treatment history,
dental health educational materials, oral health monitoring tools, and recommended visit guidance,
improving overall access to clinic services and oral health information.

       Dentists. The system improves clinical workflow by providing secure access to patient
records, an interactive digital odontogram, AI-assisted radiograph review with dentist-approved
summaries, and accurate material-usage tracking.
       Clinic Secretary. Helps efficiently encode patient information, manage appointments and
schedules, monitor inventory alerts, and handle patient concerns, significantly reducing manual
workload and facilitating secure data transfers.

       Branch Manager. Enables oversight of branch-level operations, including appointment
scheduling, queue management, patient registration, and inventory monitoring within the assigned
clinic branch.

       Clinic Owner. Enables centralized monitoring of daily patient volumes, clinic operations,
patient reports, and overall clinic performance across branches for better management and
decision-making.

       System Administrator. Maintains full control over the NgitiFy system, including user
account management for all roles, branch setup, system configuration, database integrity, audit trail
monitoring, and role-based access permissions.

       Researchers. Enhances knowledge in developing web and mobile healthcare systems,
providing valuable learning experiences in artificial intelligence integration, database management,
UI/UX design, and software development.

       Future Researchers. Serves as a solid foundation and useful reference for future studies on
innovations in dental and healthcare management systems.
1.3 OBJECTIVES

General Objective

       The primary goal is to develop and implement NgitiFy: A Dental Information Management
System with AI-Driven Patient Engagement for Dentime Dental Clinic through a web and mobile
application.



Specific Objectives
       Specifically, these are the objectives of the capstone project:
       1. To develop a centralized web and mobile Electronic Medical Record (EMR) system that
           ensures the secure synchronization and transfer of patient profiles, medical and dental
           histories, treatment history, digital odontograms, and radiograph images across multiple
           branches, featuring role-based access control restricted to the owner, authorized
           dentists, and clinic staff.
       2. To develop an Appointment and Schedule Management module integrated with a Smile
           Hub for patients that centralizes reservations from various channels, automates
           notification systems, and features AI-Driven Patient Engagement for personalized
           NgitiBot, dental health education, overall oral health management, and recommended
           visit windows.
       3. To create an Interactive Digital Odontogram integrated with an AI-Assisted Radiograph
           Review feature that enables dentists to examine radiograph quality, record and annotate
           clinical findings, link relevant tooth and treatment information, and prepare dentist-
           approved summaries for patient reference.
       4. To create a Supply and Stock Monitoring module that tracks the inventory levels of
           perishable dental items across branches and provides automated low-stock alerts. This
           module allows dentists to log material usage post-treatment for real-time manual
           deduction. It includes a report-generator dashboard that tracks daily patient volume to
           help management make branch-expansion decisions.
       5. To evaluate the system based on the following criteria:
                 a. Functionality
                 b. Reliability
                 c. Performance Efficiency
d. Compliance
e. Usability
1.4 SCOPE AND DELIMITATION

SCOPE


        The NgitiFy will function as a centralized web and mobile application, enhancing
management operations at Dentime Dental Clinic and its authorized locations. The system will
streamline patient record management, appointment scheduling, clinical support functions, and
resource tracking. The system will process Electronic Medical Records (EMR), including patient
demographic information, medical and dental records, treatment documentation, and digital
odontograms. The system will securely distribute records across branches with access controls that
permit only admins, owners, branch managers, dentists, and secretaries to access the information.


        The system enforces a role-based access control hierarchy: System Administrator, Clinic
Owner, Branch Manager, Dentist, Clinic Secretary, and Patient. The System Administrator holds full
system-wide access and is responsible for all technical configurations, user account management,
and system integrity. The Clinic Owner has cross-branch operational visibility, while the Branch
Manager oversees day-to-day operations only within their assigned branch.


        NgitiFy will provide clinical support through its Interactive Digital Odontogram system, which
enables dentists to document their findings while tracking complete tooth history. The system
includes an AI-Assisted Radiograph Review feature that presents objective image-quality
information and allows dentists to record clinical findings and prepare review summaries. This
feature serves only as decision support and does not independently interpret radiographs or provide
a medical diagnosis. The system includes an AI Patient Engagement Module composed of NgitiBot,
Dental Health Education, Oral Health Management, and the Recommended Visit Window. NgitiBot
serves as the AI Patient Care Companion by providing conversational oral health guidance and
personalized dietary recommendations within its supported scope. Dental Health Education
provides approved educational materials, while Oral Health Management allows patients to record
and monitor relevant oral health information. The Recommended Visit Window provides visit
guidance based on available patient records and oral health inputs.


The Appointment and Schedule Management Module will replace traditional calendar systems by
centralizing appointment requests, patient schedules, dentist availability, and staff schedules. The
system will provide a Smile Hub for patients together with the AI Patient Engagement Module. Within
this module, NgitiBot serves as the AI Patient Care Companion, providing real-time responses to
supported patient inquiries. NgitiFy will include a Supply and Stock Monitoring module that monitors
perishable dental supplies across different locations. This feature allows dentists to manually log
material usage, enabling the system to automatically track deductions and send low-inventory
notifications, while also producing daily patient reports that help assess the need for business
growth. The system will also be evaluated using an evaluation instrument adapted from the ISO 9126
software quality model and Jakob Nielsen’s usability attributes. The assessment covers
functionality, reliability, performance efficiency, compliance, and usability. NgitiFy's complete
dental information management system provides an efficient platform that supports clinical
decision-making while improving the patient experience.
SYSTEM FLOWCHART

       In this study, system flowcharts are presented in a swimlane format with two columns: the
left column represents user actions, and the right column represents the corresponding processes
executed by the system. This format clearly distinguishes each party's responsibilities at every step
of a given workflow. Every flowchart begins with the user as the initiating actor, consistent with
NgitiFy's event-driven nature, where a user action sets all system processes in motion. A total of
forty-eight (48) system flowcharts were constructed to document the complete operational flow of
each module and feature of NgitiFy: A Dental Information Management System with AI-Driven
Patient Engagement for Dentime Dental Clinic, covering processes ranging from user authentication
and patient registration to AI-assisted dental tools, inventory management, and system
administration functions.
